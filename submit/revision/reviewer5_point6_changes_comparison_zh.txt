【修改后英文】
ResNet50+ARC: only the Adaptive Label-Feature Relevance Construction module is introduced. The Final score increases to 76.98 (+0.79), Kappa increases by 0.44, and AUC increases by 2.29. ARC models relationships among disease labels and links each label to relevant image features.

To assess its contribution to recognizing diseases that coexist in the same eye, we compared the class-wise false positive and false negative rates of ResNet50 and ResNet50+ARC in all 66 ODIR Off-site test eyes with at least two disease labels (Table 5). For DR, adding ARC lowered the FPR by 14.29 percentage points (2/14 to 0/14) and the FNR by 1.92 points (20/52 to 19/52). For AMD, the FNR fell by 25.00 percentage points as the number of missed cases dropped from six to four among eight AMD-positive eyes. The FPR also fell by 1.72 points, with one false positive among 58 AMD-negative eyes for ResNet50 and none for ResNet50+ARC. Across the seven classes, the pooled FPR and FNR fell by 0.91 and 3.73 percentage points, respectively. These findings suggest that ARC can reduce both false positives and missed detections in eyes with co-occurring diseases by using relationships among disease labels together with the visual features relevant to each label.
