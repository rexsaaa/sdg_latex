修改后英文
Finally, the attention vectors are fed into the multi-label classifier. LayerNorm and Dropout are first applied to the class query features for lightweight feature enhancement, normalization, and regularization. Class-wise linear transformations are then used to obtain logits for each class. After Sigmoid activation, the prediction probabilities \(p^{ori}\) are obtained for each class, and \(p^{aug}\) is obtained in the same way. Both branches use asymmetric loss (ASL) [19] with positive and negative focusing parameters of \(\gamma_{+}=0\) and \(\gamma_{-}=2\), respectively, and with negative probability shifting disabled. For each branch \(v\in\{\mathrm{ori},\mathrm{aug}\}\), the classification loss is averaged over the \(B\) images in a batch and the \(N=8\) labels, then scaled by 1000:

\[
L_{cls}^{v}=-\frac{1000}{BN}\sum_{i=1}^{B}\sum_{k=1}^{N}
\left[y_{ik}\log p_{ik}^{v}+(1-y_{ik})(p_{ik}^{v})^{2}\log(1-p_{ik}^{v})\right].
\tag{10}
\]

Here, \(y_{ik}\) is the binary target for label \(k\) in image \(i\), and \(p_{ik}^{v}\) is its predicted probability in branch \(v\). Each logarithm is evaluated with its argument bounded below by \(10^{-5}\) for numerical stability. When multiple diseases co-occur, each positive label retains a focusing weight of 1. For a negative label, the weight \((p_{ik}^{v})^{2}\) reduces its loss contribution when the predicted probability of disease is low.

The total loss is \(L = L_{cls}^{ori} + L_{cls}^{aug} + \lambda L_{consist}\), where \(\lambda\) is a hyperparameter that balances the consistency term. The classification and consistency losses jointly optimize the representations of the two branches, connecting feature filtering and disease-label learning through a shared training objective and combining representation stability under appearance perturbations with multi-label discrimination.

最后，将注意力向量输入多标签分类器。首先对类别查询特征应用 LayerNorm 和 Dropout，进行轻量级特征增强、归一化和正则化。随后，通过逐类别线性变换得到各类别的 logits。经过 Sigmoid 激活后，得到原图分支对各类别的预测概率 \(p^{ori}\)，增强图分支的预测概率 \(p^{aug}\) 以相同方式得到。两个分支均采用非对称损失（ASL）［19］，正、负标签的聚焦参数分别设为 \(\gamma_{+}=0\) 和 \(\gamma_{-}=2\)，并关闭负类概率平移。对于每个分支 \(v\in\{\mathrm{ori},\mathrm{aug}\}\)，分类损失先对批内 \(B\) 张图像及 \(N=8\) 个标签取平均，再乘以 1000：

\[
L_{cls}^{v}=-\frac{1000}{BN}\sum_{i=1}^{B}\sum_{k=1}^{N}
\left[y_{ik}\log p_{ik}^{v}+(1-y_{ik})(p_{ik}^{v})^{2}\log(1-p_{ik}^{v})\right].
\tag{10}
\]

其中，\(y_{ik}\) 表示第 \(i\) 张图像第 \(k\) 个标签的二元真值，\(p_{ik}^{v}\) 表示分支 \(v\) 对该标签的预测阳性概率。为保证数值稳定性，计算对数时将其自变量的下限设为 \(10^{-5}\)。当多种疾病共现时，每个阳性标签的聚焦权重均为 1。对于阴性标签，若模型预测该疾病存在的概率较低，权重 \((p_{ik}^{v})^{2}\) 就会降低该标签对损失的贡献。

总损失为 \(L = L_{cls}^{ori} + L_{cls}^{aug} + \lambda L_{consist}\)，其中 \(\lambda\) 是调节一致性项权重的超参数。分类损失和一致性损失共同优化两个分支的表示，通过共享的训练目标连接特征筛选与疾病标签学习，将外观扰动下的表示稳定性与多标签判别结合起来。
