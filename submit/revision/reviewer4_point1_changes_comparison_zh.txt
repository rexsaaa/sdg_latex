原始英文
1. In clinical ocular disease diagnosis, a single eye may present multiple diseases simultaneously. Using a standard single-label classifier, or modeling different diseases as fully independent, can therefore lead to false positives and false negatives [10]. Multi-label recognition has also been widely studied for natural images. For example, ML-GCN [11] constructs graph relevance from label co-occurrence frequencies, making relevant labels more likely to be recognized once a given label is identified and thereby reducing false negatives. However, in multi-label ocular disease recognition, disease relevance is more complex, and methods that infer disease relevance solely from label co-occurrence frequencies may be insufficiently reliable. For multi-disease co-occurrence in fundus images, a more reliable method for label-feature relevance construction is therefore needed.

修改后英文（已通过、已落实）
1. In clinical ocular disease diagnosis, a single eye may present multiple diseases simultaneously. Using a standard single-label classifier, or modeling different diseases as fully independent, can therefore lead to false positives and false negatives [10]. Existing methods draw on different sources of information to support multi-label prediction: ML-GCN [11] uses label co-occurrence in natural images, while DCNet [12] combines information from both eyes to recognize multiple ocular diseases at the patient level. Signs of coexisting diseases may appear in different regions of the same fundus image, for example along the retinal vessels or in the macula. A model therefore needs to account for relationships among diseases and learn which local image features are relevant to each disease label.

修改后中文（已通过）
1. 在眼病的临床诊断中，同一只眼可能同时患有多种疾病。因此，采用标准的单标签分类器，或将不同疾病视为完全独立的任务进行建模，可能导致假阳性和假阴性［10］。已有方法利用不同来源的信息辅助多标签预测，例如，ML-GCN［11］利用自然图像中的标签共现关系，DCNet［12］则融合双眼信息，在患者层面识别多种眼病。共存疾病的征象可能出现在同一幅眼底图像的不同区域，例如视网膜血管沿线或黄斑区。因此，模型需要考虑疾病之间的关联，并学习哪些局部图像特征与各个疾病标签相关。
