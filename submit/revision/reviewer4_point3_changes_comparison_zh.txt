修改后英文（已通过）
Schematic illustration of the decision boundaries of a conventional model and a dual-branch model under domain shift. Panels (a1)–(a3) show the distributions of original source images, their style-augmented views, and images from the unseen target domain, respectively. In (b1), the conventional model learns a boundary that separates the two classes using only the original source images in (a1). In (b2), this boundary misclassifies some of the target samples from (a3). In (c1), the dual-branch model learns a boundary from both (a1) and (a2) that separates the two classes across the original and augmented source views. In (c2), this boundary also separates the two classes for the same target samples from (a3). By combining the two source views with CAM guidance and contrastive learning, the model aims to learn transferable disease-related features and a decision boundary that is more applicable to unseen domains.

保留的现稿英文
To learn representations that transfer across imaging conditions, the framework includes a Dual-Branch Guided Domain-Invariant Feature Extraction (DFE) module. This module is trained using only a single source domain and constructs a dual-branch structure from original source-domain images and style-disturbed augmented source-domain images, as shown in Fig. 1.a1 and Fig. 1.a2, to learn their shared representations at the feature level. Meanwhile, Class Activation Mapping (CAM) is introduced as prior guidance to encourage the model to focus on disease-related regions and suppress the learning of domain-specific noise. This enables the model to form a decision boundary that is more suitable for unseen-domain distributions (Fig. 1.c1) and to maintain stable recognition performance on unseen domains (Fig. 1.c2). In addition, CAM visualizes model-attended regions and enhances model interpretability.

为学习能够跨成像条件迁移的表示，框架引入双分支引导的域不变特征提取（DFE）模块。该模块仅使用单一源域进行训练，并利用原始源域图像及经过风格扰动的增强源域图像构建双分支结构，如图1.a1和图1.a2所示，以在特征层面学习两者共享的表示。同时，引入类激活映射（CAM）作为先验引导，促使模型关注疾病相关区域，并抑制对域特定噪声的学习。这使模型能够形成更适合未见域分布的决策边界（图1.c1），并在未见域上保持稳定的识别性能（图1.c2）。此外，CAM将模型关注的区域可视化，增强模型的可解释性。
