The training configuration is as follows. AdamW is used as the optimizer, with an initial learning rate of $4 \times 10^{-7}$. OneCycleLR is adopted as the scheduling strategy: the learning rate linearly warms up from the initial learning rate to the peak value of $1 \times 10^{-5}$ during the first 10% of epochs to help the model quickly escape local optima, and then anneals during the remaining 90% of epochs to facilitate convergence and reach a better solution. The number of training epochs is 50, the batch size is 32, and the input resolution is $448 \times 448$. The default mask threshold for dual-branch feature filtering is $\tau=0.25$, specifying the feature retention criterion between the two views after instance normalization. The default coefficients for spatial and channel CAM guidance are $\alpha=\beta=0.01$, and the default consistency loss weight is $\lambda=5$. The proposed method is trained on two RTX 4080 Super GPUs. Experimental results show that our improved baseline model, with an Off-site binocular Final score of 76.19, outperforms the ResNet50 implementation in the original ODIR paper, which reports an Off-site binocular Final score of 68.00.

To examine how feature filtering and consistency regularization affect recognition performance, the mask threshold $\tau$ and consistency loss weight $\lambda$ are varied separately in the joint DFE and ARC model under the non-private setting. With $\alpha=\beta=0.01$ fixed, $\tau$ is varied over 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, and 0.50 while keeping $\lambda=5$. The weight $\lambda$ is varied over 0, 1, 2.5, 5, 7.5, and 10 while keeping $\tau=0.25$. The two sweeps are conducted independently, with each parameter value trained in a separate run. All runs use the same data split, model architecture, augmentation strategy, and other training parameters. Each run is trained for 50 epochs with a batch size of 32 and a fixed random seed of 42.
